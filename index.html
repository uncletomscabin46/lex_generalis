About Lex Generalis

At Lex Generalis, we understand the unique challenges faced by startups, research institutions, and venture capital investors. Founded by venture capitalists and led by a team of experienced investors, entrepreneurs, engineers, and scientists, we bring a business-first approach to legal services. Our firm leverages the latest technologies to provide highly efficient, cost-effective counsel in corporate law, intellectual property, and general legal matters. Whether structuring deals, securing IP rights, or navigating complex transactions, we deliver strategic, practical solutions that align with the business goals of our clients. We don’t just advise—we partner with our clients to drive innovation, protect valuable assets, and accelerate growth.
