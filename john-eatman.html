Professional Overview

John Eatman is a Senior Associate at Lex Generalis s, where he provides comprehensive legal support across corporate transactions, venture financings, and strategic matters for both the firm and its portfolio companies.

He brings deep experience in emerging growth companies, venture capital, and complex corporate transactions, with a focus on debt and equity financings, mergers and acquisitions, and corporate governance. John works closely with startups, investors, and technology-driven businesses to support scalable growth and efficient execution of commercial and financing strategies.

Prior to joining Lex Generalis, John was a Senior Associate in the Emerging Companies and Venture Capital practice at Nelson Mullins Riley & Scarborough, where he advised clients on venture financings, M&A transactions, and general corporate matters. He has supported a broad range of companies across the technology and innovation ecosystem, with particular experience in high-growth and venture-backed environments.
