John Sears

Partner

Admitted to practice in AZ, GA · Registered Patent Attorney, USPTO

Professional Overview

John Sears is an intellectual property strategist and advisor to universities, technology companies, and investors developing advanced science and engineering innovations. With experience spanning venture capital IP leadership, university tech transfer, research laboratories, and private practice, he offers a sophisticated blend of legal, technical, and business insight.

John has overseen hundreds of patent matters involving biotechnology, chemistry, pharmaceuticals, polymers, materials science, nanotechnology, medical devices, imaging technologies, semiconductors, advanced electronics, and artificial intelligence. His pragmatic approach emphasizes aligning IP strategy with commercial goals and helping clients bring breakthrough technologies to market. A Ph.D. chemist, published author, and experienced educator, John frequently presents at major scientific conferences and remains active in AUTM

He is a Certified Licensing Professional.
